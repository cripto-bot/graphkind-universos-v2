The problem

The color refinement (1-WL) summarizes a graph into a partition of its vertices. Which operations leave that partition intact?

The next question: if the observer compresses neighborhood counts with a function f and the operation is an involution ι, in which universes (f, ι) does T4 still hold?

The result

T4(ι, f) VIVE  ⟺  ι ∈ Sₙ·K   y   (ι cambia el par ⟹ f(1) ≠ f(2))
K = {id, comp_dir, transp, comp_transp} ≅ Z₂×Z₂

The particular case: the count law

T4 VIVE  ⟺  f(1) ≠ f(2)

In the complement, a vertex with 1 neighbor in a class gets |c|−1 (which may be 2): if f collapses 1 and 2, the duality dies. What f does with 3, 4, 5… is irrelevant.

How it was found

The engine proposed and verified T4 (blind conjecture bank, 68/68), massive verification sustained it and a written proof closed the standard case. Then the lab parameterized the universe and let the data choose the law: two human hypotheses were refuted before reaching the characterization.

An autonomous construction search found the best valid witness (cycle, 99.63%) — better than the human construction. And the errors were recorded:

1. Multiset of sizes weak invariant (C6 and 2·C3 share it) → colors.
2. Comparing colors across graphs they never match → compare partitions.
3. Indices in the initial coloring trivialized everything → degrees + adjacencies.
4. Separated multisets weak k-WL → correlated pairs (= k-FWL).
5. The wrongly built Shrikhande it was the Rook twice → caught by is_isomorphic.

Five own bugs caught by controls, none announced before verifying. The method is part of the result.
